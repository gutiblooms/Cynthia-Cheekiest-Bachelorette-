Cash: Bring USD to exchange + pesos for taxis/cenotes. Budget ~2,000–3,000 pesos cash pp. ATMs in Tulum centro.
⚠️ Important Notes
🌿
Sargassum: May is seaweed season on Tulum beaches. Taboo has a pool so Sunday is fine. Beach days may vary — check day-of conditions.
💧
Cenote rules: Apply reef-safe sunscreen 20 min BEFORE entering water — required at both cenotes. No regular sunscreen allowed.
🎉
Pro tip: Tell EVERY venue it's a bachelorette when you arrive/book — you'll get extra attention, sometimes free shots or decorations.
🌙
Night safety: Don't bike on paths at night. Stick to roads. Always negotiate taxi price before getting in after dark.
🛒
Grocery store: Chedraui or Super San Francisco in Tulum centro — ~10 min taxi from villa. Buy Thursday on arrival.
